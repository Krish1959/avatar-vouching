Privacy Policy

Avatar Agentic AI Pte Ltd (UEN 202557356E), trading as AVAG.AI ("we", "us"). Effective 1 November 2026.

1. What we collect

Through the Avatar Vouching Agentic AI service we collect: accounting company name, customer company name, contact email address, and the ledger files, output-format samples, shared-folder links and notes you choose to submit.

2. Why we collect it

Solely to verify your email, receive your files, perform the audit-vouching engagement you requested, and contact you about it. We do not sell, rent or use your data for advertising.

3. Where it is stored

Submitted files and details are stored in AVAG.AI's private Google Drive account. Confirmation emails are sent via Google's Gmail service. Access is limited to authorised AVAG.AI personnel and the automated systems that process your engagement.

4. Google user data

This application uses Google APIs only on AVAG.AI's own Google account, to store submitted files in Google Drive and send email via Gmail. It does not access the Google accounts of visitors. If you share a Google Drive link with us, we only copy the files you have shared. Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Retention

Data is kept for the duration of the engagement and any period required by law or professional standards, then deleted. You may request earlier deletion at any time.

6. Your rights

Under Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA) you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, or withdraw consent.

7. Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS and stored in access-controlled Google infrastructure. Email verification codes expire after 10 minutes.
