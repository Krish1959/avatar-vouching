Privacy Policy

Avatar Agentic AI Pte Ltd (UEN 202557356E), trading as AVAG.AI ("we", "us"). Effective 1 November 2026. Last updated 7 November 2026.

Alpha test notice. During the current alpha test, submission and result folders are shared as "anyone with the link" so testers can open them without a Google account. Anyone who has a link can view and download its contents, so please do not forward links. The verified-email-only access described in sections 3 and 5 will apply when the alpha test ends.

1. What we collect

Through the Avatar Vouching Agentic AI service we collect: accounting company name, customer company name, contact email address, and the ledger files, output-format samples, shared-folder links and notes you choose to submit. We also record your IP address and browser type with each submission, for security and audit-trail purposes.

2. Why we collect it

Solely to verify your email, receive your files, provide the AI-assisted vouching support you requested, and contact you about it. We do not sell, rent or use your data for advertising, and your data is not used to train AI models.

3. Where it is stored and who can access it

Uploaded files and submission details are stored in AVAG.AI's private Google Drive account. Confirmation emails are sent via Google's Gmail service. Internal access is limited to authorised AVAG.AI personnel and the automated systems that process your request.

🔒 Shared-folder links — read-only, in transit only. Files you provide through a shared Google Drive link are read directly from your link into memory, sent over encrypted connections to our AI providers (OpenAI, Google, Anthropic) for analysis, then discarded. We never copy them to our storage. Under our paid API terms, providers do not use your data to train their models. Only your results report is saved. We keep the link itself and a list of the file names and sizes we found, so the results can show which files were examined. You may revoke your link once the run completes.

Access to output files: your results (Google Doc and Google Sheet) are shared only with the contact email address you verified, with view and download access but no online editing. General link access is restricted, so a forwarded link will not open for anyone else. Your input files are not shared back with anyone.

4. AI processing

To produce your results, the contents of your submitted files are processed by the following AI service providers through their business API services:

  • OpenAI (ChatGPT models)
  • Google (Gemini models)
  • Anthropic (Claude models)

Files are sent to these providers within each request; we do not use their file-storage services. Under their API terms, these providers do not use submitted data to train their models. All three providers maintain a presence in Singapore. Where your data is processed outside Singapore, we rely on the providers' contractual commitments to protect it to a standard comparable to the PDPA, in line with the PDPA's Transfer Limitation Obligation. By submitting files you consent to this processing.

Any file the service cannot examine (for example an unsupported file type, a file over a size limit, or a folder we cannot open) is listed in your results as "Not examined", so you can see exactly what was and was not reviewed.

5. Google account needed to open results

Results are delivered through Google Drive. The contact email you enter must be linked to a Google account to open them. If your organisation uses Google Workspace, your office email already works. If it uses Microsoft 365 or another provider, you can link a free Google account to your existing office email (no Gmail address needed) at accounts.google.com/signup by choosing "Use my current email address instead". Access is granted only to the exact email you verified; forwarding the results email to another address will not grant access.

6. Google user data

This application uses Google APIs only on AVAG.AI's own Google account, to store uploaded files in Google Drive, share your results with your verified email, and send email via Gmail. It does not access the Google accounts of visitors. If you share a Google Drive link with us, we only read the files you have shared, for the duration of your review, and we do not copy them. Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. Retention

Uploaded data and results are kept for the duration of your request and any period required by law or professional standards, then deleted. Files from shared links are not retained. You may request earlier deletion at any time.

8. Your rights

Under Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA) you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, or withdraw consent.

9. Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS and stored in access-controlled Google infrastructure. Email verification codes expire after 10 minutes.
